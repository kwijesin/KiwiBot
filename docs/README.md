## Conventions

- Decisions go in `decisions/` (copy `0000-template.md`). Required parts: title, status, date, tags, decision, related decisions.
- Anything consciously put off goes in `decisions/deferred.md`.
- Pages marked **Placeholder** will be filled in by the self-improvement loop as decisions are made.
